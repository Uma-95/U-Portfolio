👋 About me

It all started with college fests and club events: pulling off fests, managing club juniors, and making sure things didn’t fall apart five minutes before go-time. I didn’t realize it back then, but all that behind-the-scenes chaos was my gateway into product. What started as organizing events turned into a curiosity spiral: why do some things just work? What makes people show up, stay engaged, and actually enjoy the experience?

At AlgoUniversity I was suddenly in the thick of it, planning national bootcamps like the Graph Programming Camp, running feedback loops and building cohorts. Today I work on product and growth at Future AGI, an LLM and voice-agent evaluation platform, with the same instincts and better tools.

Curiosity has always been my compass. The mission is simple: build products that feel thoughtful, human, and genuinely useful, by staying curious, listening with intention, and obsessing over the little details.
