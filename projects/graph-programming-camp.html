ℹ️ About the project

💁

Context & Goal

AlgoUniversity aimed to tap into college communities with high CS interest to promote its advanced programming offerings. The Graph Programming Camp was designed as a free value-first entry point, a workshop on the topic of graphs run by a Codeforces Master, targeted at students from top-tier colleges (IITs, NITs, IIITs, etc.).

The main goals were:

  • Introduce Algouniversity to students and create top-of-funnel trust via a quality graph theory camp
  • Identify high-performing students for premium mentorship programs.

As a Product Manager at AlgoUniversity, I was tasked with overseeing the execution of this Graph Programming Camp. The objective was to efficiently onboard participants, ensure maximum engagement throughout the camp, and convert top participants into leads for further courses. The project aimed to streamline the process across the onboarding, registration, and sales stages post the bootcamp to enhance user experience and achieve growth.

🚀 Timeline & Execution Overview

👨‍💻

We ran this campaign in 4 phases:

1. Onboarding → 2. Registration & Shortlisting → 3. Camp Delivery → 4. Sales Conversion Owned communications, coordination, and user experience across all touchpoints.

1️⃣ ONBOARDING: Campus Collab Outreach

🎯

Strategy:

  • Leverage placement cells & tech society heads as points-of-contact (POCs)
  • Build micro-groups for each college to manage ops closely

Operations & Tools:

  • Emails with a value proposition to the official placement cells and coding clubs (from the official AlgoU domain)
  • Airtable to track college outreach & replies
  • Main mode of communication with the college was through WhatsApp groups created per college: CollegeName <> AlgoUniversity

Key Actions steps:

  • Sent hiring/collab pitch emails to the potential colleges.
  • Jumped on calls with interested stakeholders.
  • Discussed the bootcamp and further steps. Cross-checked with the schedules of students and the bootcamp dates to ensure no clashes with their semester exams.
  • Added POCs to official WA groups, briefed them on intent, expectations & timelines
  • Shared onboarding content and registration forms for students through the student Point of contact through the official college emails.

My Contributions:

  • Drafted and sent targeted emails to placement cells and society heads from official AlgoU domain
  • Handled calls with stakeholders to pitch the camp, clarify expectations, and set timelines
  • Created and managed 1:1 WA groups with POCs (CollegeName <> AlgoUniversity) for direct updates
  • Sent clear, purpose-driven intro messages to POCs and personalized onboarding kits (FAQs, camp posters, sample content)
  • Aligned internal team (design, content) to push college-branded promotional creatives

2️⃣ REGISTRATION & SHORTLISTING: Funnel Activation

🎯

Strategy:

  • Use college WA groups + emails to drive student sign-ups
  • Personalize messaging by year/branch for relevance
  • Build hype with the designed marketing strategy

Assets used:

  • Google Form with Airtable-backed tracker
  • Canva-designed custom posters (editable per campus)
  • WA/email scripts tailored to each POC

Key Actions steps:

  • Sent registration forms with Bit.ly links to track engagement per campus
  • Analyzed form responses daily → year-wise & branch-wise gaps flagged
  • Deployed FAQ reminders if second-year or overall traction was low
  • Final shortlist released ~24 hours before the event; all shortlisted students added to a new WA group

My Contributions:

  • Managed the end-to-end registration funnel using Google Forms + Airtable tracking
  • Created Bit.ly links for every campus to monitor traction and identify drop-offs
  • Customized WhatsApp messages + emails by college, year, and branch to target underserved groups
  • Ran participation audits daily → if second-year CS students were low, sent specific second-year FAQs + reminders
  • Sent targeted nudges for underrepresented groups to re-engage.
  • Designed and sent entry passes (WA + email) only to shortlisted candidates with call-to-action messages.
  • Coordinated camp WA groups, shared pre-camp hype content, and schedule teasers
  • For the offline bootcamp:
    • Conducted a whole separate event to collect their entry passes, notebooks, and other collectibles like badges, which triggered FOMO across unregistered groups
    • Ran mini-quizzes on DSA concepts (Graphs, DFS, etc.) and rewarded with a free coffee for creating buzz amongst the campus students.
    • Gave out collectible rewards for chocolate problem winners that created a buzz loop, quiz winners posted pictures on their socia lmedia handles.

🎯 Key Learnings

  • Streamlined Communication Drives Participation: Consistent follow-ups with college POCs via WhatsApp and email significantly boosted registrations and engagement.

  • Customized Content Enhances User Experience: Tailoring messages, registration forms, and challenges, like the "Chocolate Problem"(where the students are given one problem as homework after the class and whoever solves it gets a chocolate as a reward), improved participation and retention.

  • Strategic Timing Increases Responsiveness: Observing patterns in student exam schedules and focusing on weekend sessions led to better response rates.

  • Gamification Encourages Engagement: Incorporating problem-solving challenges during the camp kept participants motivated and actively involved.

  • Data-Driven Prioritization Maximizes Conversions: Analyzing attendance, performance, and application data helped identify high-potential leads for effective follow-ups.

  • Collaboration Across Teams Is Key: Coordinating with multiple stakeholders aka colleges, POCs, mentors, and internal teams, was essential to the program's success.

  • Adaptability Enhances Outcomes: Extending deadlines and adjusting approaches based on real-time feedback improved registration and conversion rates.

  • Post-Event Momentum Is Crucial: Maintaining engagement after the event through mentor calls and personalized follow-ups ensured successful admissions.
