ℹ️ About the project

🎯

This was a strategic product-growth roadmap built for Creator Circle, a platform built for creators who want to collaborate with niche-aligned peers, save time using AI-powered video tools, and grow a community that aligns with their content values. The core idea behind Creator Circle is to help creators move away from algorithm-chasing and burnout. Instead, it offers them filtered visibility, meaningful 1:1 connections, and fast editing tools to streamline their creative process. For this roadmap, I was asked to design a detailed six-month user acquisition blueprint. The brief included an ambitious goal: map out how Creator Circle could go from 0 to 100,000 users in half a year, keeping both creators and viewers in mind. The focus was on low-CAC growth, user-driven virality, and systems that convert signups into long-term platform advocates.

While this was not a completely executed project, I approached it like a product manager would, building systems around activation, feedback loops, retention, and product-led scale.

🚀 Timeline & Execution Overview

⏰

This strategy was designed to play out over six months, divided into three sequential growth phases. Each phase focused on a different scale, different product behavior goals, and different go-to-market mechanics.

  • Phase 1: 0–100 Creators
  • Phase 2: 100–10,000 Creators
  • Phase 3: 10,000–100,000 Users (creators + viewers)

Each phase worked as both a learning and execution loop, intended to validate assumptions early, build momentum mid-way, and unlock viral network effects toward the final stretch.

1️⃣ Onboarding: Early Adoption and Validation

🗣

This phase was all about finding the first 100 creators who would not only use the product but also actively talk about it and help shape its early identity. I structured this like a micro go-to-market sprint, where every action was designed to maximize insight, reduce churn, and build early trust.

The target audience here was smaller creators with around 1,000–5,000 followers, people who hadn’t fully “made it” yet but were ambitious, growth-focused, and community-driven. The value proposition offered to them was not just tools, but status: early access, co-creation opportunities, and visibility within a curated group.

The approach centered around direct outreach. I proposed manually DM-ing 500 creators across Instagram, YouTube, and LinkedIn, with custom scripts based on their niche and journey. Each script emphasized early access, creator matching, free AI tools, and the chance to shape the platform’s trajectory.

To make the experience feel real and tailored, I also mapped out a lightweight onboarding toolkit. This included a starter guide, editable content templates, audience trend reports for their niche, and a personalized welcome from the team. The goal was to make every early user feel like a founding member.

Every interaction would be tracked in Airtable: who we reached out to, how they responded, what they clicked, and when they dropped off. Feedback would be collected through Google Forms and onboarding DMs, then rolled into weekly iteration cycles.

I also proposed offering early adopters special access to post-level analytics, profile-based highlights, and custom growth resources as a way to reward their engagement and build retention from the start.

2️⃣ Registration, Activation, and Creator-Led Scaling

💡

Once the first 100 creators were onboarded, the next challenge was to scale the acquisition loop to 10,000 creators, without losing the signal, connection, or value of the product experience. This phase was designed to lean heavily into creator behavior, gamified systems, and UGC.

The centerpiece of this stage was a gamified onboarding experience I titled the “Grow Your Circle” challenge. It was built as a 7-day journey where each day nudged users to explore one core feature of the platform. The challenge began with setting up a profile, progressed into watching or joining collabs, and culminated in creators posting a reel using the AI tool and tagging another creator to bring into the platform.

Each task was designed to serve a product goal, whether that was activation, feature adoption, or network expansion. Users who completed the challenge would unlock free AI credits, premium tools, and a visible badge on their profile.

To turn every creator into a growth channel, I built out a referral system using a tiered rewards model. Each creator would get a unique referral link (tracked via FirstPromoter or any UTM tool). For every successful referral, they would earn "Circle Coins," which could be redeemed for advanced editing tools, premium templates, and profile boosts. The highest-performing referrers would get spotlighted on the platform, giving them visibility and status as community leaders.

Parallel to this, I recommended testing small-batch paid ads, but not before validating messaging through organic creator content. I mapped out a system where high-performing user-generated reels (showcasing AI tool use or community stories) would be turned into ads. These ads would run on Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Pinterest, with micro budgets to start. Only ads with strong organic CTRs and solid post-click behavior would be scaled.

Throughout this phase, metrics were closely tied to creator actions: How many creators completed the onboarding challenge? How many reels were created using the AI tool? How many referrals came from top creators? Where were the drop-offs happening?

All these questions fed directly into product decisions, from redesigning certain parts of the onboarding experience to improving clarity on the 1:1 call feature.

3️⃣ Viral Growth, Retention Mechanics, and Community Loops

🔥

By the time the platform hit 10,000 creators, the growth strategy needed to shift from hand-holding to habit-building. This phase was designed to optimize for LTV, boost daily usage, and unlock self-sustaining viral loops.

To achieve that, I proposed turning every core product action into a passive distribution mechanism. One example was watermarking every AI-generated reel with a “Made with Creator Circle” tag. When someone viewed that content and clicked the tag, they’d be taken directly to the signup page. This turned user content into free, contextual advertising, without relying on paid impressions.

I also proposed layering in a full reward system using a gamified currency called Circle Coins. Users could earn these coins by performing key actions, posting reels, booking 1:1 calls, inviting others, or joining challenges. Coins could then be redeemed for exclusive features, merch, creator perks, or brand collaborations. This turned routine product usage into a retention loop.

To re-engage dormant users, I designed a system of push-based nudges: trending audio alerts, new collab matches, or reminders tied to recent activity. The idea was to keep the product top-of-mind without being intrusive, using data to personalize timing and context.

Another key part of this phase was community-powered visibility. I suggested introducing niche-based “Creator Challenges” tied to calendar events or content trends, like a Mental Wellness challenge during World Mental Health Day, or a Productivity Collab Week for side-hustle creators. These campaigns would both drive UGC and give creators a reason to stay connected to the ecosystem.

Finally, I recommended collaborating with creators who had small but loyal followings, offering either monetary rewards (₹500–₹1,000 per campaign) or barter-based perks like free access to premium tools. These creators would act as ambassadors during viral pushes, helping bring in newer audiences at a lower cost than traditional influencer partnerships.

Outcomes (Projected Targets from Assignment Proposal)

💭

Since this was a proposal and not a live project, the following outcomes were set as key success indicators had the strategy been executed:

  • 300 to 500 signups from the onboarding challenge within the first few weeks
  • Activation rate above 60% based on 7-day challenge completion
  • 150+ reels posted using the AI tool in the early stages (first 2 months)
  • 75+ 1:1 calls booked between creators
  • 200+ pieces of branded UGC using challenge hashtags
  • Referral-based signups accounting for 30% of total new users
  • CAC kept low by relying on UGC, referrals, and organic loops
  • Daily usage tied directly to reward systems and community nudges

🎯 Key Learnings

🔥

While this was a strategic assignment and not an implemented project, building this case study clarified several principles I would bring to any early-stage growth role.

The biggest learning was that creators are not just users, they’re stakeholders in the brand. They don’t just want tools; they want connection, direction, and context. So onboarding them isn’t about walking them through features, it’s about helping them feel seen and set up for success.

Second, gamification is not a gimmick when tied to real product value. When reward systems are aligned with creator goals like growth, recognition, and collaboration, they create momentum, not friction.

Third, product-led growth doesn’t mean doing everything inside the product. It means building systems where every user action creates a ripple, a reel that drives a click, a collab that drives trust, a referral that builds the next loop. Every action should lead to another.

Lastly, emotional design is a growth advantage. Challenges, coins, badges, they’re not just retention hacks. They’re how creators build identity inside a platform. If done well, they turn your product from a tool into a place.
