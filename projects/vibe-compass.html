ℹ️ About the project

💁

POV: The Vibe Compass is a self-initiated project where I began building a digital-first brand focused on emotional support, self-reflection, and mental wellness for Gen Z and young millennials. The goal is to offer relatable, comforting content that helps users process emotions, glow up mentally, and feel seen, without sounding preachy. The project is currently in its early build phase, with foundational work on branding, audience research, and content strategy already done.

🎯 Key Learnings (To Date)

  • Clarity Comes from Action: Starting with a clear emotional mission helped streamline all branding and content decisions.
  • Audience First Always Wins: Focusing on shared struggles (spirals, burnout, emotional overwhelm) made it easier to shape relatable content.
  • Minimal Cost ≠ Minimal Value: Found ways to build and test ideas using free tools and platforms, proving scrappy can still be strategic.
  • Brand Tone Matters: Establishing a unique voice, a mix of cozy bestie energy, sarcasm, and soft reality checks will became a key differentiator from traditional wellness content that sounds so hustle-bro-ish.
