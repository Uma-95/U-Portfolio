Case Study: Product Analytics Using Mixpanel About the project

💁

Randosando is a fictional food delivery company based in NYC that caters specifically to keto, vegan, and gluten-free diets. Established a year ago, the company went viral with 5000 users and has grown to a team of 15 employees.

The goal is to utilize the available data and identify features and key points that will drive sustainable growth without compromising food quality and service, ensuring that Randosando remains one of the best food delivery apps by effectively scaling operations, meeting user needs, and fostering customer satisfaction.

In this case study, I explored the domain of product analytics by utilizing Mixpanel to analyze a dataset of 5,000 customers. The primary objective was to gain actionable insights into customer behavior, acquisition, and retention. The exercise involved creating a comprehensive taxonomy, framing key business questions, and leveraging Mixpanel’s tools to generate reports and visualizations.

📌

Objective

The goal of this case study was to:

  • Understand customer behavior and identify trends.
  • Analyze key metrics such as acquisition, retention, and profitability.
  • Create a dashboard for easy monitoring and decision-making.

🪜 Approach

📎

1. Data Preparation

  • The dataset comprised 5,000 customer records, including details of transactions, customer journeys, and interactions with the product.
  • Pre-processed the data to ensure compatibility with Mixpanel’s analytics tools.

2. Taxonomy Development

  • Defined key events and properties within the dataset to standardize tracking.
    • Examples of events: “Order Placed,” “Sandwich Received,” “Feedback Provided.”
    • Examples of properties: “Customer ID,” “Order Value,” “Time to Delivery.”

3. Framing Key Questions

Focused on framing business-relevant questions, such as:

  • What is the daily active user (DAU) trend over the last month?
  • What is the conversion rate at each funnel step in the customer journey?
  • Which acquisition channels yield the highest retention rates?
  • How does profitability vary by customer segment?

4. Funnel Analysis

  • Created funnels to track the customer journey, including:
    1. Sandwich selection.
    2. Order placement.
    3. Delivery receipt.
  • Measured drop-off rates at each step to identify areas for improvement.

5. Retention and Acquisition Metrics

  • Calculated acquisition metrics to understand the effectiveness of marketing efforts.
  • Analyzed retention metrics to determine how frequently customers returned and

re-engaged with the product.

6. Dashboard Creation

Designed a Mixpanel dashboard to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs):

  • Daily Active Users (DAU): Displaying trends over time.
  • Profit Analysis: Highlighting profit generated in the last month.
  • Funnel Analysis: Visualizing conversion rates at each stage of the customer journey.
  • Retention Metrics: Showing cohort-based retention trends.

✨ Key Outcomes

📎
  • Actionable Insights: Identified key pain points in the customer journey, such as high drop-off rates during order placement.
  • Improved Understanding: Gained hands-on experience in defining taxonomies, framing analytical questions, and interpreting Mixpanel reports.
  • Effective Visualization: Built a dashboard for real-time monitoring of user engagement, profitability, and retention.

✅ Key Learnings

  • The importance of a well-defined taxonomy in enabling meaningful analysis.
  • How to leverage Mixpanel’s capabilities to generate insights that drive business decisions.
  • The value of product analytics in improving customer experience and optimizing business performance.

Conclusion

This case study reinforced my skills in product analytics, enabling me to explore customer behavior, measure business-critical metrics, and present insights effectively. The experience provided a practical understanding of Mixpanel and its potential to drive data-informed strategies.
